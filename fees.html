Out-of-Network Benefits

Many clients are surprised to learn that they have out-of-network mental health benefits, which may significantly reduce the cost of therapy.

To make the process simple, I partner with Thrizer. After entering your insurance information, Thrizer can estimate your reimbursement, submit claims on your behalf, and send your reimbursement directly to you.

For clients who prefer an even simpler option, Thrizer also offers an optional service that allows eligible clients to pay only their estimated out-of-pocket cost at the time of each session while Thrizer waits for reimbursement from your insurance company. (This optional service includes an additional fee through Thrizer.)

My hope is to make using your out-of-network benefits as seamless and stress-free as possible.

Good Faith Estimate

Under the No Surprises Act, healthcare providers are required to provide clients who are uninsured or choosing not to use insurance with a Good Faith Estimate of the expected cost of services.

If you have any questions about fees, payment, or your out-of-network benefits, I'm always happy to help before your first appointment.
