Services

Emotionally Focused Couples Therapy

Relationships can become stuck in painful cycles of conflict, distance, or misunderstanding. Emotionally Focused Therapy (EFT) helps couples identify these patterns, better understand one another's emotional needs, and rebuild the secure connection that allows relationships to thrive.

Interracial Couples Therapy

Every relationship brings together two unique life experiences. Interracial couples often navigate additional layers of culture, identity, family expectations, and communication. Therapy provides a supportive space to explore these experiences together while strengthening trust, understanding, and emotional connection.

Individual Therapy

Sometimes the relationship that needs the most attention is the one you have with yourself.

I work with adults experiencing:

  • Anxiety
  • Perfectionism
  • Low self-esteem
  • Burnout
  • Relationship concerns
  • Life transitions

I would love to walk alongside you in self-exploration to help you build self-esteem so you can feel confident in your authentic self. Whether you're working through people-pleasing, navigating a career change, or simply feeling stuck, therapy can help you build a stronger foundation of self-trust.

Together, we'll explore the patterns that may be keeping you stuck and help you create a life that feels more centered, connected, and authentic.

Therapy isn't about having all the answers. It's about understanding your patterns, building awareness, and making intentional choices that align with the life you want to create.
